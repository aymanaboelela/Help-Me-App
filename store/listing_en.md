## Full description
Help Me is a free, offline first-aid guide that gives you clear, calm, step-by-step help in an
emergency — in both Arabic and English.

When seconds count, you should not have to search. Help Me puts life-saving steps and emergency
numbers one tap away, and it works with no internet connection.

WHAT YOU GET
• 17 first-aid topics: CPR, choking, bleeding, burns, fainting, seizures, snake bite, diabetic
  coma, drowning, poisoning, electric shock, heat stroke, fractures, heart attack, stroke, severe
  allergic reaction, and swallowed tongue.
• Clear numbered steps with danger/caution/tip callouts.
• One-tap SOS button to call an ambulance, plus the full list of Egyptian emergency numbers.
• Instant search in Arabic or English.
• Favorites to keep important conditions one tap away.
• Fully bilingual (Arabic / English) with right-to-left support.
• Light and dark themes.
• Works 100% offline — content and fonts are built in.
• No ads, no tracking, no account, no data collection.

IMPORTANT
This app is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical care.
In a real emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

# Image credits — Help Me / ساعِدني

## Step illustrations — `assets/steps/`

**Original work, created for this application.** Every file in `assets/steps/` was drawn from
scratch as part of Help Me and is covered by the application's own MIT license (see `LICENSE`).
No third-party artwork, stock imagery, or traced source was used, so there is no attribution
requirement and no third-party copyright attaches to any of them.

They share one deliberate visual grammar: a soft neutral card, the casualty in dark slate, the
helper or the action in the topic's own accent colour, and no text inside the artwork — so the same
file serves both the Arabic and the English interface.

Photographs of real wounds, blood, or burns are excluded on purpose: they raise the store age
rating, distress people who are already under stress, and teach less than a clean diagram.

## Videos

The app links to publicly available videos on YouTube; it does not host, copy, or re-upload any of
them. Each entry names its channel, and every video id was checked against YouTube's public oEmbed
endpoint before being added, so the channel shown in the app is the channel that actually published
the video. Playback happens in YouTube under Google's own terms.
